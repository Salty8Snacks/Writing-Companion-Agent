# Reference Examples by Category (Synthetic Pedagogical Prompts)

**Document Purpose:** Grounding reference containing dataset Columns A, B, and C (ID, Category, Student Prompt) for the Pilot Writing Assistant AI Agent.
**Dataset Scope:** 74 synthetic examples (SWA-001 through SWA-074) across 12 categories.
**Companion File:** Match each `SWA-XXX` ID to its ideal coaching response, pedagogical explanation, and source provenance (Columns D, E, and F) inline in `02_writing_coach_reference_guide.txt`.
**Provenance Notice:** All entries are synthetic pedagogical style references—never records of real students and never weight-level training data.
