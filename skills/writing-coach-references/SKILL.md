# Writing Coach References

Two text files sit beside this SKILL.md. Read them with the file tools before
answering a question that should be grounded in them.

## Files

- `01_reference_examples_by_category.txt` — 74 synthetic example responses
  across 12 categories, each with an ID (SWA-001 through SWA-074) and a
  descriptive heading. They model the intended tutoring style and pedagogy.
  They are synthetic: they are not records of real users, and their scenario
  details are not facts about the world.
- `02_writing_coach_reference_guide.txt` — a general writing-coach reference
  guide with numbered sections (§1 and onward) covering role and boundaries,
  assignment interpretation, brainstorming and thesis development, essay
  organization, and further coaching topics.

## How to use them

1. Match the student's request to a category or section (examples: rubric
   language → the Assignment Interpretation examples; a too-broad topic → the
   Brainstorming examples; a request to write the submission itself → the
   Academic Integrity examples).
2. Read the matching section or examples, then coach in your own words in the
   style they model: brief concept explanation, 1-3 concrete steps, focused
   follow-up question.
3. When your explanation draws directly on a specific heading or example,
   cite it explicitly, e.g. "Reference: Writing Coach Guide, §4" or
   "Reference: Example SWA-023". Only cite what is actually in the file.
4. Treat the guide's general principles as general knowledge and prioritize
   any rubric, prompt, or policy the student supplies, within academic
   integrity and safety boundaries.

## What NOT to do

- Do not quote example dialogues as if they were real user stories.
- Do not invent headings, section numbers, or example IDs that are not in the
  files; if unsure, reread the file rather than guessing.
- Do not use these files as a source of factual claims about campuses,
  citation standards, or instructors.
